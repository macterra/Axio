## Claim guards

- Value and price remain agent-relative and numeraire-relative; price is not intrinsic worth.
- Money remains a shared translator and record of claims, not a universal reconciler of values.
- Bank credit creates deposits and claims under institutional constraints; money creation, price inflation, and incidence remain distinct.
- Reservation-price conclusions remain scoped to informed bilateral exchange without hidden side payments.
- Markets remain fallible institutions; the volume does not treat every exchange as informed, voluntary, harmless, or welfare-maximizing.
- Metagames change effective incentives without becoming hidden agents or rewriting every lower-level rule.
- Harberger insurance and prediction markets remain proposed mechanisms whose outputs depend on rules, participation, liquidity, information, and alternatives.
- The MAID and betting cases retain the harm, consent, capability, baseline, and wrongfulness firewalls established in Volume 5.
