# Phase 7 Cross-Volume Patterns, Selection, and Cultural Agency Audit

Baseline: July 27, 2026, after the sacredness, meaning, and chosen-commitments audit.

## Result

Volume 1 Chapters 8 and 24 retain pattern identification, physical implementation, provenance, and dynamical identity. Volume 8 Chapters 2–5 retain the cultural hierarchy: schema, population, institution, lineage, differential propagation, agency-qualified organization, and egregore remain distinct.

Two later recaps overextended the selection model:

- Volume 8 Chapter 5 said the same dynamics run at every scale and identified the growth of a private framework with the birth of a collective thought-form. The revision treats the private case as an analogue and possible precursor. Collective lineage additionally requires transmission through other carriers and institutions.
- Volume 9 Chapter 15 first acknowledged that selection for meaning-production requires causal evidence, then called meaning-supporting features the traits that got religion selected and made meaning necessary for survival. The revision retains the organism metaphor while treating meaning-production as one possible contribution among doctrine, family transmission, community, institutions, coercion, and historical chance.

The remaining recaps preserve the governing distinctions:

- Pattern type, physical token, resemblance, and causal lineage are separate.
- Selection claims specify variants, mechanisms, differential outcomes, environments, and horizons.
- Fitness is comparative propagation or persistence, not truth, merit, value, progress, or agency.
- Schemas, populations, institutions, and agents remain separate units.
- Egregore and attractor language remains analogical unless the claimed mechanisms or dynamics are supplied.
- Agency remains with people and independently qualified organizations; recurrent aggregate outcomes alone do not create a chooser.
