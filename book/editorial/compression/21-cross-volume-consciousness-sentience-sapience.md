## Result

The revision makes the argument substrate-neutral and identifies the actual dispute: whether welfare and sovereignty are commensurable quantities on one scalar ledger. Sentience can support welfare concern without establishing sapient authorship; sapient authorship can support sovereign standing without proving sentience.

The later Volume 1, 3, 4, 5, and 7 recaps otherwise preserve the required firewalls:

- Recursive self-modeling and functional organization remain evidence within a consciousness theory, not proof of phenomenality.
- Functional valence remains distinct from felt valence.
- Cognition, fluency, and agency evidence do not settle sentience automatically.
- Sentience supports patienthood and welfare concern without implying sovereignty.
- Sapience names reflective authorship and is not defined to require sentience.
- Protection and status claims remain calibrated to axis-specific evidence.
