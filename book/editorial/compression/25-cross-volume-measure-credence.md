# Phase 7 Cross-Volume Measure and Credence Audit

Baseline: July 27, 2026, after the patterns, selection, and cultural-agency audit.

## Result

Volume 1 Chapter 9 retains Measure as normalized Born weight for a specified event sector relative to a state, event definition, conditioning record, and coarse-graining. Volume 2 Chapter 11 retains Credence as an agent's epistemic probability and makes every Measure–Credence bridge conditional on accepted physical, self-location, typicality, and decision assumptions.

Volume 1 Chapter 16 previously recapped the QBU by saying every quantum event forks the world into real branches, each carrying Measure. That formulation turned emergent record histories into countable event-by-event worlds and attached Measure to each branch rather than to a specified event sector.

The revision restores the canonical hierarchy:

- Decoherent histories are emergent and grain-relative rather than uniquely countable worlds.
- Measure belongs to specified outcome or record sectors under an explicit model.
- Microscopic differences can amplify into macroscopic divergence, but not every interaction guarantees a distinct high-level outcome.
- Credence remains an epistemic quantity whose scope extends beyond quantum branching.
- Measure may constrain likelihoods or rational expectation only through stated bridge assumptions.
- Neither physical weight nor epistemic confidence supplies moral value, harm, responsibility, or permission by itself.

The remaining Volume 1, 2, 3, 5, and 7 recaps preserve the distinction among physical weight, agent uncertainty, self-location, policy-conditioned distributions, and normative application.
