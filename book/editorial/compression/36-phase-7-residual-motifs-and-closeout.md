# Phase 7 Closeout: Residual Motifs

## Scope

This final inventory tests the ledger entries that remained generic after the twenty-five bounded cross-volume families:

- water boiling as Conditionalism's simple-truth example;
- retired branch-deletion and branch-revaluation language; and
- cathedrals, sacred light, and disenchantment as framing motifs.

It also checks whether another compression family is supported by evidence or would amount to editing for recurrence alone.

## Findings

### Water boils at 100°C: retain; no cut

Volume 2 Chapter 2 owns the full example. It uses altitude, pressure, purity, calibration, and ordinary conversational compression to introduce Conditionalism.

Chapter 4 contains one later sentence with a different job: after classifying underdetermined and nonsensical statements, it uses the fully conditioned boiling claim as a control that has determinate reference and can be evaluated. Removing the callback would save no repeated argument and weaken the contrast.

No other manuscript chapter revives the example.

### Branch deletion and revaluation: retired; no cut

The manuscript no longer describes agents as deleting, creating, destroying, selecting, or globally reallocating branches or Measure. The one search hit says explicitly that observation “does not create the branch.” Physical and ethical chapters instead use event sectors, policy-conditioned distributions, prospective exposure, and causal comparison.

The Phase 5 retirement therefore remains complete after the Phase 7 Measure/Credence and harm/coercion audits.

### Cathedrals, sacred light, and disenchantment: retain; no cut

The two Volume 8 cathedral uses do not spend Volume 9's ending:

- Chapter 1 turns a thin historical kernel into the “cornerstone of cathedrals,” completing its legend-scale image.
- Chapter 5 names cathedrals and stock exchanges as pre-AI products of cultural amplification.

Its final letter's “I did not trade a cathedral for an empty lot” is the culminating personal formulation. Earlier references establish the question, the secular-sacred proposal, and the functional religion bridge; none duplicates the envoi.

### Repetition ledger: closed

Every core-definition row now records a Phase 7 decision. Every recurring-example row is either audited in Phase 7 or explicitly retired under the Phase 5 reconciliation and rechecked here. The remaining recurrence is deliberate independent-volume orientation, domain application, worked calculation, or motif.

## Phase 7 exit assessment

The evidence supports closing the phase:

- all nine volumes received chapter-level editing before Phase 7;
- Volume 4 and Volume 7 received complete continuous compression passes;
- the remaining local-sequence audit selected and completed the Volume 1 Chaos relay;
- twenty-five bounded cross-volume families established ownership, compressed demonstrated duplication, and repaired definition or calibration drift;
- no chapter was merged or cut merely to meet a word target;
- repeated full builds produced 253 pages with internal-link validation;
- reviewer cadence restores preserved signature lines when calibration edits cooled the voice.

The final residual search found no section that survives solely through repetition. Further family selection from the same ledger would now optimize for novelty rather than evidence.

## Decision

Phase 7 is complete. Preserve the residual motifs. Begin Phase 8 with framing material so the preface, book introduction, volume introductions, conclusions, titles, and cross-volume transitions describe the manuscript that survived editing.
