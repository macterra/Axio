# Phase 7 Evolution, Selection, and Progress Audit

Baseline: July 28, 2026, after the games, metagames, and incentives audit.

## Result

Volume 1 Chapter 18 retains the book's canonical variation-and-selection mechanism for creativity, while Volume 5 Chapter 18 and Volume 8 Chapter 3 retain the wider safeguards: a selection account must specify variants, retention or transmission, differential outcomes, environment, and horizon. Selection is retrospective differential persistence, not a chooser, purpose, universal objective, or guarantee of progress.

Two later recaps exceeded that claim ceiling.

Volume 1 Chapter 15 described evolution as a universal thermodynamic arms race and the universe's mechanism for developing increasingly effective agents. It also treated intelligence and progress as optimization results. The revision makes increased prediction and control conditional on reproductive advantage in particular lineages, names simplification, parasitism, and extinction as counter-trajectories, and makes progress an agent's evaluation rather than evolution's direction.

Volume 7 Chapter 23 asserted that efficient, stable hierarchies outlasted deliberative groups and that evolution optimized group survival. Its setup also called every proposed force well documented and standard equipment. The revision presents context-sensitive deference as a plausible biological-and-cultural selection hypothesis, preserves the chapter's threat-and-submission mechanism, and calibrates the differing evidence bases while removing the unsupported universal comparison and singular group-level objective.

The remaining Volume 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, and 9 applications preserve the firewall. They use evolution for attributed arguments, conditional functional support, cultural transmission, or locally specified selection pressures; none requires a universal ascent toward truth, consciousness, agency, morality, religion, or political hierarchy.

## Protected-material ledger

- Preserve Volume 1 Chapter 18's variation-and-selection account and its distinction between undirected variation and criterion-relative filtering.
- Preserve Volume 3's conditional evolutionary support for consciousness and its separation of function from phenomenality.
- Preserve Volume 8's cultural selection machinery and the distinction among persistence, truth, value, merit, and agency.
- Do not turn differential persistence into foresight, lineage-specific elaboration into a universal trend, adaptation into progress, or a contested ancestral scenario into a settled political inheritance.
