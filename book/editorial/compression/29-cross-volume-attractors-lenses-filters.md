# Phase 7 Attractors, Lenses, and Filters Audit

Baseline: July 28, 2026, after the evolution, selection, and progress audit.

## Result

Volume 8 Chapter 3 retains the terminology boundary. In a specified dynamical model, an attractor is a state or region toward which nearby trajectories tend and within which they remain; the claim requires a state space, dynamics, and basin. Elsewhere, attraction, gravity, curvature, filters, and lenses may name analogies only when the prose supplies the causal mechanism and does not borrow mathematical necessity.

Four recaps crossed that boundary.

- Volume 3 Chapter 18 called every disciplinary lens an active attractor. It now names the actual tool-bias mechanism—biased attention, framing, and hypothesis choice—and reserves the technical term for a modeled dynamical claim.
- Volume 8 Chapter 26 said the volume's three attractor scales were views of one phenomenon rather than a shared metaphor, contradicting Chapter 3's explicit warning. It now treats them as a family of analogies with related but separately specified feedback structures.
- Volume 9 Chapter 11 called terminal valuing an attractor without any convergence dynamics. Its heading now names a terminus in a chain of instrumental reasons.
- Volume 9 Chapter 15 called recurrent cognitive susceptibilities and deliberately chosen virtues attractors. It now names the cognitive features, recruitment mechanism, and chosen orientations directly.

The remaining physical, alignment, cultural, and meaning uses either supply state spaces and dynamics, explicitly identify analogy, attribute the term to another proposal, or perform local mechanism-bearing work. Filters name retention or information-selection operations; lenses name declared interpretive perspectives. Neither implies convergence by itself.
