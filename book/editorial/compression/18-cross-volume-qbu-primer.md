# Phase 7 Cross-Volume QBU-Primer Audit

Baseline: July 27, 2026, after the viability/persistence audit.

## Scope

This audit tests the repetition-ledger instruction to keep the full Quantum Branching Universe construction in Volume 1 Chapter 8 and use only compact, jurisdiction-specific bridges elsewhere.

The audit searched the complete manuscript for QBU, Quantum Branching Universe, Everettian, branching, decoherence, Born weight, and Measure. It read the Volume 1 Chapters 8–17 sequence, Volume 2's optional probability module, and the concentrated ethics applications in Volume 5 Chapters 18, 20, and 21.

The ownership test asked:

1. Does a later passage reconstruct the graph, ancestry relation, Pattern Identifiers, or Strong/Weak provenance distinction?
2. Does it preserve branches as emergent, approximate record histories at a declared coarse-graining rather than countable fundamental worlds?
3. Is QBU necessary to the local argument, or would general Credence and decision language suffice?
4. Does it keep Measure physical, Credence epistemic, and every bridge between them conditional?

## Canonical ownership

Volume 1 Chapter 8 owns the QBU construction: a model-relative DAG of selected decoherent record histories, ancestry, Pattern Identifiers, provenance, and the unweighted skeleton. Chapter 9 adds Measure, Vantage, and Branchcone. Chapters 10–17 then apply that machinery to causality, probability, choice, and identity rather than reintroducing it.

No later chapter reconstructs the full formal object. The front matter and glossary give compact orientation and explicit status labels; Volume 1's earlier chapters foreshadow the model while linking forward to its canonical statement.

## Independent-volume bridges

Volume 2 Chapter 11 contains the longest later primer, but it performs necessary work for an independently readable probability volume. It states only four commitments needed by the optional module, marks branches as emergent and non-countable, and sends the reader to Volume 1 for the formal construction. Chapters 14–16 use that module to address the Measure-to-Credence bridge, sampling assumptions, and self-location rather than repeating the graph.

Volume 5 Chapter 20 labels its QBU description optional and leaves the decision-level ethics intact. Chapter 21 gives one compact bridge before developing responsibility, promises, rescue, Vantage-indexed obligation, and a speculative identity application. Both state that Measure does not supply moral classification or duty.

**Decision:** Retain the independent-volume bridges; no compression is warranted.

## Ownership correction

Volume 2 Chapter 14 previously said the QBU's “physical credentials” were laid out in *Measure and Credence*, assigning ownership to the local probability primer rather than the physics volume. The same sentence also used “everything happens” after a narrower laboratory setup, language that can be misread as modal plenitude rather than nonzero-amplitude outcomes under a specified state.

The revision links the canonical Volume 1 construction directly and states that every represented nonzero-amplitude outcome in the example occurs in a decoherent component. It then frames the probability problem as the absence of one selected outcome, not the occurrence of every imaginable event.

**Decision:** Correct ownership and scope; retain the chapter's opening argument.
