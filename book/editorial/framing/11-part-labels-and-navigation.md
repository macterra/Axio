# Part labels and navigational handoffs

Phase 8 audited the manifest's part labels and the prose that introduces, closes, or hands off between parts. Most labels accurately describe structure. Six framing points required correction because they restored claims already bounded in the reviewed manuscript.

## Applied corrections

| Location | Previous framing | Reviewed framing | Boundary restored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vol. 1, ch. 18 handoff | Creativity “populates those branches with novelty” | Creativity produces novel models and actions within the branching representation | Policy and creativity can make causal differences without generating futures or Measure. |
| Vol. 3, ch. 29 opening and close | AI “will obey” the same dynamics, “will do the same,” and instantiates a pattern every informational superpower follows | Earlier upheavals supply a comparison; AI may instantiate an analogous pattern and may produce analogous turbulence | Historical analogy supports hypotheses, not a deterministic cultural law or forecast. |
| Vol. 5 introduction | Part VI “is the destination” | Part VI is the constructive culmination | The sequence culminates in a proposed ethics under an openly chosen premise, not a destination supplied by history or structure. |
| Vol. 7 Part VII label | *From Monopoly to Protocol* | *Monopoly, Anarchy, and Protocol* | Protocolized governance is tested as a proposal; the part is not a one-way historical conversion. |
| Vol. 7 introduction | Part VII “is the destination” | Part VII is the final constructive sequence | Axiocracy and protocol governance remain proposals whose gains can reverse, not civilization's objective terminus. |
| Vol. 9, ch. 9 handoff | Meaning is constructed rather than inherited or discovered; affluence removes the excuse for meaning arriving from outside | Agents inherit, encounter, revise, and make meaning; affluence removes some external pressures | Authorship revises and extends received significance rather than requiring meaning to originate wholly inside one will. |
