# Phase 8 Framing Inventory and Sequencing

## Purpose

Phase 8 revises the manuscript's promises after structural, argumentative, reconciliation, glossary, compression, and line editing are complete. Framing must describe the book that survived those passes rather than preserve an earlier prospectus.

This phase covers:

- the preface and book introduction;
- nine volume introductions;
- volume conclusions and final chapter handoffs;
- chapter titles and subtitles;
- cross-volume transitions; and
- status and record-authority language.

It does not reopen a settled argument merely to make framing more dramatic. When framing and body disagree, the body governs unless the disagreement exposes a substantive defect that requires a separately scoped audit.

## Framing tests

Each framing claim must answer five questions:

1. **Ownership:** Does it point to the chapter or volume that now owns the claim?
2. **Register:** Does it distinguish theorem, evidence, interpretation, proposal, commitment, and metaphor?
3. **Scope:** Does it preserve the conditions and open problems retained in the body?
4. **Sequence:** Does it promise the argument in the order the reader will encounter it?
5. **Voice:** Does it keep the book's directness without restoring an overclaim removed in Phase 7?

The Phase 7 firewalls remain binding. In particular, framing may not:

- turn Measure into world-counting or agency into branch creation;
- turn agent-relative value into an objective moral fact;
- turn persistence or selection into merit, progress, intention, or destiny;
- turn harm into wrongfulness or influence into coercion;
- turn direct force into violence or coercion by synonym;
- turn analogy into a supplied dynamical or computational model; or
- present an open contradiction, empirical conjecture, or chosen commitment as closed.

## First-cluster finding

The preface and book introduction had four framing mismatches:

- the manuscript was described as mostly draft although every published chapter is in review;
- the ethical arc sounded deductive once agents replaced the cosmos as the source of value;
- the culture summary made truth categorically irrelevant to transmission;
- the blog was called the home of the full argument despite the preface correctly describing it as a historical notebook containing superseded positions.

The revision names the manuscript's current review state, states the chosen agency-protection premise, makes accuracy one transmission factor among several, and establishes record authority: chapters govern the synthesis, papers govern formal detail, and blog posts preserve the historical path.
