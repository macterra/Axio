# Bounded chapter labels

Phase 8 audited chapter titles and subtitles against the reviewed bodies rather than treating inherited blog headlines as verdicts. Seven labels materially exceeded the claims their chapters now defend.

## Applied corrections

| Location | Previous label | Reviewed label | Boundary restored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vol. 3, ch. 7 | *Consciousness Explained* | *A Candidate Architecture of Consciousness* | The chapter presents MST as a hypothesized subsystem and research proposal, not a completed explanation of phenomenality. |
| Vol. 3, ch. 13 | *The Sentience Metric* | *Tests for Sentience* | The chapter offers three theory-dependent evidentiary tests, not a validated scalar. |
| Vol. 3, ch. 35 | *The Future Is Extropian* | *The Extropian Crucible* | The chapter is participant history about an intellectual milieu, not a prediction that the future has one ideology. |
| Vol. 4, ch. 6 subtitle | *Safety by architecture, not by learned values* | *Alignment by architecture, not by learned values* | Structural alignment supplies constraints; the volume explicitly denies that structure guarantees safety. |
| Vol. 5, ch. 20 | *Risk Is Harm* | *When Risk Is Harm* | Only a foreseeable, attributable, material worsening relative to an appropriate baseline qualifies as prospective harm. |
| Vol. 5, ch. 27 subtitle | *Why evil persists in any world worth living in* | *Why preventing evil can endanger agency* | The chapter presents a tension under the framework's chosen conception of agency, not a theorem about every worthwhile world. |
| Vol. 7, ch. 25 subtitle | *Nations are not agents; interests are not primitives* | *National interests are interpretations, not primitives* | A group label alone does not establish a subject, while attributable institutional agency remains possible. |

Live manuscript cross-reference labels were updated where titles changed. Historical source identifiers and filenames remain unchanged.

## Anchor and route safety

- No filename, slug, URL, or Markdown heading changed.
- Titles and subtitles are front-matter metadata rendered as page labels, not fragment identifiers.
- Cross-reference destinations are unchanged.
- The build's link validator remains the final route check.

## Deliberate no-edits

Provocative labels remain where the body clearly frames and earns the provocation rather than silently asserting it. Examples include *Why Zombies Don't Evolve*, whose opening states the conditional functionalist dispute, and *The Authoritarian Default*, whose chapter openly argues for that named thesis rather than disclaiming it in the body.
