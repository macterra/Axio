# Phase 8 Framing Audit: Minds, Alignment, and Ethics Introductions

## Scope

This audit compares the Volume 3–5 introductions with the reviewed chapters they frame. The cluster carries three distinct burdens: evidence about consciousness and machine agency, the changing evidentiary registers of a live research program, and an ethics whose normative force begins in a chosen commitment rather than a fact read from nature.

## Volume 3

The introduction already preserves the distinctions established in the edited volume:

- fluent behavior does not prove consciousness, sentience, persistence, or agency;
- the Modeler-Schema and Agency-Model theories propose functional architectures and correlates, while their phenomenal identity claim remains controversial;
- the sentience tests are theory-dependent evidentiary windows rather than a universal metric;
- agency is assessed at the level of the whole deployed system; and
- catastrophic-risk and governance claims require additional empirical and normative premises.

No substantive framing change is needed. The closing status alone still called the volume a draft despite its `review` status and the manuscript-wide author-review state. It now matches the record.

## Volume 4

The introduction already separates theorem, experimental result, engineering demonstration, interpretation, and aspiration; limits the Reflective Stability Theorem to its assumptions and implementation; gives negative results and non-claims equal billing; preserves the formal papers as the technical record; and freezes the represented program state at February 23, 2026.

One summary said the Reflective Coherence Thesis proposes that reflection “selects for coherent interpretation.” The defining chapter makes a narrower tendency claim: under specified semantic openness and epistemic constraint, reflection tends to eliminate some objectives that cannot remain intelligible and action-guiding. It neither supplies a selection dynamics nor guarantees a coherent or benevolent endpoint. The introduction now states that scope directly.

## Volume 5

The introduction already passes the normative and definitional guards:

- value remains relative to valuers;
- conditional objectivity means public assessability under disclosed premises rather than agent-independent authority;
- Phosphorism is chosen and revisable;
- coercion, consent, property, harm, and justified force remain distinct proposed definitions;
- persistence supplies a descriptive constraint, while an explicit commitment supplies normative force;
- the innocent-instrument invariant is proposed rather than discovered; and
- the aggressor-contaminated-field contradiction remains visible and limits completeness.

No manuscript change is needed.

## Decision

The three introductions now promise their edited volumes at the correct evidentiary and normative registers. No title, part structure, cross-link, research cutoff, or canonical ownership change is needed.
