# Title and subtitle no-edit inventory

Phase 8 reviewed all 252 titled source records and all 243 subtitles after the seven bounded corrections recorded in `09-bounded-chapter-labels.md`. The test was semantic, not stylistic: does the label state or imply more than the reviewed body defends?

## Method

Each label was checked against:

1. the chapter's opening scope and definitions;
2. qualifications added during Phases 6 and 7;
3. the conclusion's actual verdict;
4. live cross-reference usage;
5. whether a technical term is asserted literally, supplied with dynamics, attributed to a source under examination, or explicitly marked as analogy;
6. whether a provocative headline names the thesis argued in the chapter or contradicts a disclaimer inside it.

The seven mismatches found by this process were corrected in the preceding cluster. The remaining labels require no edit.

## Attractor follow-up

Two labels received an additional check because Phase 7 restricted unexplained attractor language.

- **Volume 8, chapter 26 — *The Oldest Attractor*.** The chapter supplies the recurrence mechanism: a perverse Schelling point reinforced by precedent and shared expectations under stress. It also states that the volume's three scales are “a family of analogies with related feedback structures,” not one mathematically established phenomenon.
- **Volume 9, chapter 4 subtitle — *the agency-attractor*.** This is the name of Joscha Bach's proposal being granted for examination alongside the Trinity and Being-itself. The chapter criticizes its unspecified objective and concludes that the defensible remainder is a coordination equilibrium. Attribution plus deflation makes the label accurate.
