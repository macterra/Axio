## Relative-time dispositions

Most of the 66 riskier time hits are structurally timeless:

- indexicals used as examples (*today*, *tomorrow*, *now*);
- contrasts such as “yesterday's solution becomes today's selection pressure”;
- historical comparison (“people today live with conveniences kings could not buy”);
- descriptions of a modeled current state inside an example;
- attributed quotations whose wording must not be silently modernized;
- explicit dates attached to data, legislation, hearings, or events.

Four classes deserve separate treatment.

### Retain dated baselines

The World Bank's 2024 poverty line, the April 2026 Senate hearing, the 2025 WTO monitor, the 2021 MAID expansion, and similar claims name their observation date or source. They are durable statements about what a dated record said, even if later records differ.

### Replace edition-relative placeholders

Two sentences use the mutable manuscript as their clock:

- Volume 6 Chapter 18: “As of this edition” before the scheduled March 17, 2027 MAID rule.
- Volume 7 Chapter 9: “As of this edit” before a description of pending criminal proceedings.

Both should name a calendar date. That will turn a silently expiring assertion into an auditable historical snapshot without pretending later legal developments are known.

### Scope market-state claims

Volume 3's claims about what no system “on the market today” instantiates, what “today's models” are, and what current AI intimacy amounts to can age faster than the surrounding functional argument. They should be tied to the evidence and systems under discussion rather than made universal through an unversioned *today*. The book's agency and sentience tests should survive a product cycle.

### Retain rhetorical and comparative time

“Today's opportunities are yesterday's outcomes,” “today's loss can become tomorrow's gain,” and comparable lines are temporal relations, not claims about publication date. Removing their indexicals would damage the examples without improving durability.

## Superseded-claim dispositions

The Phase 7 firewalls remain intact in the canonical and applied claims:

- harm remains a material setback distinct from wrongfulness;
- coercion remains a credible conditional threat used to obtain compliance, distinct from influence, force, and violence;
- selection remains descriptive rather than progress or truth;
- civilizational progress remains evaluative rather than teleological;
- narrative fitness can compete with truth rather than replacing it;
- operating-system language remains bounded as analogy;
- patterns and institutions do not become hidden agents by label.

Searches do, however, expose a small residual reification family:

| Location | Residual | Why it needs repair |
|---|---|---|
| Vol. 8 ch. 4 | “patterns, not people, are the real units of cultural selection” | Contradicts the canonical token/type/agent hierarchy; pattern types are analyst-specified units, while people remain causal and responsible agents. |
| Vol. 3 ch. 25 | escalation “finds its attractors”; systems “crave the upvote”; “the parasite is the pattern” | The feedback mechanism is present, but these sentences turn its compressed description back into dynamical necessity, appetite, and an abstract actor. |
| Vol. 7 ch. 33 | coordination “thickens the very attractors”; “Leviathan is therefore an attractor” | No state space, dynamics, or basin is supplied locally; the actual claims are stabilizing relationships and a recurrent large-scale failure mode. |

Other attractor uses are sound:

- physical and neural cases invoke actual dynamical systems;
- Volume 4's phase treatment supplies trajectories and stability behavior;
- Volume 8 explicitly defines the technical requirement and labels its three cultural uses a family of analogies;
- *The Oldest Attractor* supplies a Schelling-point coordination mechanism and repeats the analogical boundary;
- Volume 9 attributes and then deflates another author's agency-attractor proposal.

The sharp “Capture is an attractor” close in Volume 8 Chapter 18 is retained because it is downstream of the volume's explicit analogical guard and names the chapter's demonstrated feedback structure. The issue is not the word itself but unearned technical or agentive implications.
