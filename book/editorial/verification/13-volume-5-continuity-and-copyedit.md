# Volume 5 continuity and copyedit

Date: 2026-07-29

Scope: continuous reading of the Volume 5 introduction and all 27 chapters, with particular attention to the is/ought boundary, the harm/coercion/consent/force definitions, evidentiary attribution, rival-theory accuracy, and the explicit Chapter 25 limit.

## Continuity result

The load-bearing sequence holds. The volume states its agency-protection premise as chosen, keeps descriptive harm separate from wrongfulness, distinguishes coercion from influence and direct force, treats consent as scoped authorization, and leaves the contaminated-field conflict openly unresolved. The later viability construction does not derive normativity from persistence.

Four surrounding passages exceeded those disciplines. Chapter 4 briefly made costly behavior constitutive of value after correctly calling sacrifice defeasible evidence. Chapter 8 treated diagnostic phrases as proof of institutional motives. Chapter 16 inferred Parfit's method and motive more strongly than the text under review could establish. Chapter 22 compressed several rival traditions into inaccurate formulations and claimed to resolve the human-shield case that Chapter 25 deliberately leaves open.

## Applied repairs

- **Chapter 4:** sacrifice remains a sharp public signal of enacted priority without becoming the whole of value. Duress reveals avoidance under threat rather than endorsement; cross-agent comparison remains conditioned on means and alternatives; observers retain an interpretation burden.
- **Chapter 8:** three vivid field-guide entries now turn on contradictory conduct and missing costly evidence rather than certainty about what institutions or speakers privately mean.
- **Chapter 16:** the Parfit critique targets the evidential independence of convergence among selected and reconstructed theories. It no longer diagnoses a philosopher working backward or attributes revisions to a hidden protective motive.
- **Chapter 22:** utilitarianism, Kant's humanity formula, and contractualism receive accurate minimal statements before Axio marks its disagreements. The libertarian comparison now says the definitions clarify hard cases while exposing the unresolved human-shield conflict.
