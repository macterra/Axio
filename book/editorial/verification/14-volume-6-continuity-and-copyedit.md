# Volume 6 continuity and copyedit

Date: 2026-07-29

Scope: continuous reading of the Volume 6 introduction, 25 chapters, and coda, with particular attention to empirical causation, exchange assumptions, counterfactual quantification, dated claims, and the boundary between market mechanisms and universal laws.

## Continuity result

The volume's central discipline holds: value remains agent-relative; prices are signals rather than measures of intrinsic worth; money, wealth, capital, currency, and credit remain distinct layers; incentive and market claims are increasingly conditioned on institutions, information, bargaining, externalities, and exit. The coda correctly leaves governance questions open rather than treating coordination as salvation.

Four residual clusters exceeded those boundaries. Chapter 3 presented one contested origin sequence as the history of money and made currency a universal reconciler of value. Chapter 4 inferred reservation values from every recorded market price and reduced planning failure to one calculation mechanism. Chapter 21 labeled an unvalidated nuclear counterfactual illustrative, then described hand-selected mortality and trillion-dollar placeholders as conservative estimates. Chapter 22 retained categorical causal prose around later paragraphs that already marked optionality, housing, and policy as hypotheses requiring evidence.

## Applied repairs

- **Chapter 3:** debt-first evidence now complicates rather than simply reverses the barter story. Money can function as bookkeeping across multiple historical paths; acceptance and comparability are bounded to relevant networks and priced opportunities.
- **Chapter 4:** reservation-price inference is scoped to informed, voluntary bilateral exchange. Recorded prices can also reflect auctions, bargaining, market power, regulation, urgency, and coercion. The socialist-calculation passage identifies loss of decentralized price formation without claiming that administrative information is nonexistent or that one mechanism explains every planning failure.
- **Chapter 21:** mortality and electricity figures are explicit scenario arithmetic. The geopolitical and lost-industry entries are labeled unsupported placeholders, consistent with the chapter's own reproducibility warning and sensitivity table.
- **Chapter 22:** development remains associated with lower fertility without becoming an exceptionless law. Children retain later relational and economic contributions; optionality, digital substitution, housing, price, and social institutions remain candidate mechanisms rather than a single civilizational chooser. Observed fertility is not redescribed as one authentic collective preference.

## Retained structure

- The coat-and-ticket layer model and the price-as-signal core remain intact.
- The market-failure, monopoly, incentive, and governance chapters retain their institutional boundary conditions.
- The nuclear chapter retains its full illustrative table, sensitivity handles, pro-nuclear argument, and complete-system close.
- The prosperity chapter retains the optionality thesis, mortgage/village image, non-coercion boundary, and final line.
- The AGI chapter retains comparative advantage under scarcity while denying that it guarantees human wages, safety, or extinction.
- No title, subtitle, heading, internal link destination, source record, or status changes.
