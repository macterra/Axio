## Continuity result

Formal results are conditional on definitions and assumptions; proof-of-concept experiments report their harnesses and tested ranges; the running artifact establishes implemented mechanisms without claiming benevolence, open-world security, or a solved alignment problem. The coda accurately records the program's revisions and negative results.

Three residual transitions exceeded those registers. ## Applied repairs

Once encoded, a gate can operate independently of ordinary optimization; architecture does not itself choose consent or harm as its content. Ontological reinterpretation is a risk under changing models rather than an inevitable fate.

- **Chapter 8:** the anchor result is scoped to the implemented protocol and threat model. ACV still establishes temporal dependency and detects represented replay/precomputation strategies; it does not prove that no fabricated anchored surface is possible under every adversary.
