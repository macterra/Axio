# Volume 9 continuity and copyedit

Date: 2026-07-29

Scope: continuous reading of the Volume 9 introduction and all 20 chapters, with particular attention to the boundary between naturalistic explanation and metaphysical conclusion, chosen commitment and objective value, analogy and mechanism, developmental model and diagnosis, and speculative future history and teleology.

## Continuity result

The closing volume is unusually explicit about its registers. Sacredness is offered as a protected practical role rather than a discovered essence; transcendent experience is separated from metaphysical inference; the Credo states first-person commitments; future minds and the Great Unfolding remain conditional constructions; and both closing letters preserve successor and reader agency.

Three bridges exceeded those limits. Chapter 6 presented a three-factor diagnostic metaphor as exact arithmetic. Chapter 13 turned Kegan's interpretive ideal types into population-wide claims about biological cost, developmental incapacity, and others' motives. Chapter 14 first marked its three maps as analogical, then repeatedly collapsed myth, phenomenology, information theory, agency, and participation into one demonstrated referent and mechanism.

## Applied repairs

- **Chapter 6:** the multiplicative sagency formula is identified as a diagnostic model rather than a validated cardinal scale. The zero-factor warning, three failure modes, and closing synthesis remain.
- **Chapter 13:** Kegan's stages remain interpretive ideal types and a developmental ideal used by the chapter, not a diagnosis of all believers or a biological law. Costs and conformity incentives are scoped to circumstances, and the letter to the ancestors illustrates rather than proves Stage 4.
- **Chapter 14:** the three maps retain a family resemblance without becoming one literal substrate. The chaos-form-coherence cycle remains a psychological and cultural schema and a physical analogy, not one cross-register mechanism. Agency and participation become mutually supporting descriptions within the proposed synthesis rather than the only mind the universe permits.

## Retained structure

- Chapter 6 retains the multiplication image, all three named failure modes, the power/stewardship argument, the three-line closing remedy, and “Sagency is what wisdom looks like once it has hands.”
- Chapter 13 retains the Kegan ladder, “Obedience scales more cheaply than judgment,” the heretic/house image, the inheritance/maturity aphorism, the full ancestor letter, and its final handoff to the two envoi letters.
- Chapter 14 retains the three-map table, the five-stage cycle, constructor figure, jazz improviser, “Meaningness offers the how without the why. Axio offers the why without the how,” and the two-line meaning/valuing formulation.
- Chapters 18–20 retain the disclosed meta-myth, the machine-descendant benediction, and the book's final sentence.
- No title, subtitle, heading, internal link destination, source record, or status changes.
