# Volume 1 continuity and copyedit

Date: 2026-07-28

Scope: continuous reading of the Volume 1 introduction and all 28 chapters, with particular attention to handoffs among the physical agency argument, the QBU model, the speculative Chaos sequence, and the later normative program.

## Continuity result

The volume's governing boundary holds. Parts I–III present the kybit as a model-level measure of an attributed distributional shift, restrict its thermodynamic lower bound to an equilibrium-reference realization, and keep QBU language conditional on a declared state, event partition, Vantage, and causal model. Parts IV–V mark the Chaos ontology, coherence filters, and realization principle as speculative proposals rather than consequences already derived from physics.

One chapter-level handoff failed that boundary. Chapter 6 retained source-era language claiming a one-to-one conversion between kybits and variational free energy even though Chapters 3, 4, and 13 now explicitly deny a universal exchange rate. The repair keeps active inference as a useful comparison while separating its quantities, mechanisms, and evidentiary role.

## Applied repairs

- **Chapter 1:** generic constructors are no longer described as agency-building forces, and preservation or expansion of agency is identified as the framework's chosen standard rather than a value supplied by physics.
- **Chapter 5:** the Matching Pennies inference is limited to fixed action and execution conditions; increased agent capacity replaces an unqualified progress direction.
- **Chapter 6:** active inference becomes a partial structural comparison and research program, not a one-to-one derivation or currency conversion. The second and third laws now match the bounded formulations in Chapters 3–4.
- **Chapter 18:** recursive selection and revisable criteria are presented as a candidate account of open-ended creativity rather than a sufficient settled algorithm. The AlphaZero description now names self-play and search-guided move selection.

## Retained handoffs

- Chapter 13's honest ledger remains the canonical close of the physical/QBU bridge: no conjuring, no universal joule-per-kybit theorem, and no ethical conclusion supplied by physics.
- Chapters 19–22 distinguish representation from realization and filters from physical constructors.
- Chapters 23–28 preserve the speculative register and explicitly leave the realization relation and Modal Realization Principle open.
- No title, subtitle, heading, link destination, source record, or status changes.
