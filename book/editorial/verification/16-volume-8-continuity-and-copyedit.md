# Volume 8 continuity and copyedit

Date: 2026-07-29

Scope: continuous reading of the Volume 8 introduction and all 27 chapters, with particular attention to cultural-selection mechanisms, pattern reification, historical evidence, motive attribution, controlled comparison, and the volume's stated prohibition on mind-reading.

## Continuity result

The volume's canonical model holds. Patterns are analyst-specified configurations implemented in carriers and performances; selection claims require variants, transmission or retention, differential outcomes, environment, and horizon; people remain the choosing and responsible agents. The capture chapters increasingly distinguish observed asymmetry from motive, and the Taboo Test closes by defining its result as narrowed inquiry rather than mind-reading.

Three applied clusters exceeded those limits. Chapter 1 called three selected historical examples a controlled demonstration, treated narrative as the whole action-relevant self, and overstated the uniformity of the historical evidence. Chapter 20 inferred a platform population from one reported timeline, declared online migration structurally identical to white flight, and diagnosed users' motives and developmental stage without evidence. Chapter 25 identified the private purpose of land acknowledgments where institutional conduct supported only a substitution or inaction claim.

## Applied repairs

- **Chapter 1:** Arthur, Socrates, and Jesus are differently evidenced historical kernels in an illustrative comparison, not a controlled demonstration. Narrative remains a major mechanism of self-representation alongside embodiment, memory, control, relationship, and causal continuity.
- **Chapter 20:** the BlueSky observation becomes a reproducible test proposal rather than a platform-wide verdict. The white-flight analogy is scoped to selective separation moralized as protection while retaining differences in history, coercion, material harm, and legitimate exit. Kegan's Socialized Mind remains a candidate interpretive mechanism, not a diagnosis inferred from platform choice. Purity drift becomes a conditional feedback loop with named interrupters.
- **Chapter 25:** land acknowledgments can substitute ceremony for claims and remedies regardless of private intent. Institutional convenience and missing second steps remain observable; evasion as purpose and near-universal practical failure do not.
