# Volume 2 continuity and copyedit

Date: 2026-07-28

Scope: continuous reading of the Volume 2 introduction and all 25 chapters, with particular attention to the boundaries among Conditionalism, formal limit results, Bayesian updating, the optional QBU module, and the interpretation-independent closing sequence.

## Continuity result

The volume's main architecture holds. Chapters 1–10 develop the epistemology without requiring QBU; Chapters 11–16 identify the branching treatment as an optional module and keep Measure distinct from Credence; Chapter 17 resumes the interpretation-independent argument; Chapters 22–24 correctly place model construction and criticism outside conditionalization alone.

Three residual handoff problems required repair. Chapter 1 overstated philosophy's exclusive priority and stated Gödel's second incompleteness theorem without its scope conditions. Chapter 17 converted an evolutionary analogy into literal identity and an unbounded-progress conclusion. Chapter 22 briefly treated QBU Measure as the interpretation-independent ground of an ordinary coin probability.

## Applied repairs

- **Chapter 1:** first-order domain methods are distinguished from reflexive criticism without claiming that only professional philosophy can perform it. Gödel's result now carries its consistency, effective-axiomatization, and arithmetic-strength conditions. Conditionalism's rules are proposed reconstructions rather than rules every agent already runs.
- **Chapter 17:** conjecture, criticism, and retention remain an evolutionary analogy; ideas are proposals handled by agents and institutions rather than evolutionary entities. Continued improvement remains an invitation, not a theorem of progress without limit.
- **Chapter 22:** ordinary physical and statistical anchors for a fair-coin model no longer depend on QBU; sector Measure is identified as an additional representation conditional on adopting that module.

## Retained handoffs

- Chapter 5 explicitly distinguishes the formal results of Tarski, Gödel, Turing, and Chaitin from Conditionalism's philosophical interpretation.
- Chapters 11–16 keep state, event decomposition, self-location, and the Measure-to-Credence bridge explicit and disputed.
- Chapters 18 and 25 preserve coherence and agency as openly chosen commitments rather than objective values derived from epistemology.
- Chapter 24's final division remains intact: Bayes within models; criticism between them.
