## Chapter 23: market incentives and evasion

## Chapter 31: the AGI race

- the safety-through-acceleration strategy *helped intensify* competition rather than single-handedly triggering it;

- familiar institutional incentives replace “civilization, considered as a whole, optimizes for action”;
- the “civilizational attractor” becomes a self-reinforcing coordination problem;
- actors who see AGI as both dangerous and decisive *can* treat acceleration as defense, producing a race no single institution controls, rather than inevitably producing one nobody can switch off.

The chapter still says the fear of the race helped create the race, and the Cassandra payoff remains: “the prophet is not merely ignored; the prophecy becomes fuel.”
