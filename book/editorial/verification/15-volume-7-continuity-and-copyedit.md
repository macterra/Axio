# Volume 7 continuity and copyedit

Date: 2026-07-29

Scope: continuous reading of the Volume 7 introduction and all 36 chapters, with particular attention to coercion classification, institutional and collective agency, historical generalization, governance teleology, and the evidential register of political psychology.

## Continuity result

The volume's core firewalls hold. Taxation and slavery share a coercive-extraction dimension without becoming morally equivalent; classification does not settle legitimacy; collective labels do not create one chooser; proposed governance protocols do not inherit history's direction; and the defense verdict remains explicitly open. The final medical case remains fiction with dated legal framing rather than a claim of reportage.

One technical claim and one connected political cluster exceeded those guards. Chapter 4 treated money-supply expansion as identical to a uniform loss of purchasing power. Chapter 23 stated a deliberately bounded evolutionary hypothesis, then repeatedly summarized authoritarianism as a universal, pre-installed human fate and partisan betrayal as clockwork prediction. Chapters 24 and 27 inherited that stronger register. Chapter 28 described markets, open protocols, and peer review as eliminating politics and enforceable leverage rather than redistributing and constraining them.

## Applied repairs

- **Chapter 4:** policy-driven dilution can redistribute purchasing power, but monetary expansion, consumer-price inflation, and incidence are separated. Credit, velocity, output, money demand, expectations, and timing remain empirical parts of the mechanism.
- **Chapter 23:** *default* now names a recurrent risk produced by interacting psychology, institutions, material conditions, and ideology. The six-factor architecture, discount metaphor, key image, and default/discipline contrast remain; universal history, deterministic evolutionary psychology, and clockwork prediction do not.
- **Chapters 24 and 27:** opening and handoff summaries now carry Chapter 23's bounded register. Cross-system comparisons remain evidence without becoming controlled experiments or proof that liberalism wins every case and loses every constituency.
- **Chapter 28:** markets, protocols, and scientific replication remain anti-capture mechanisms. Competition, switching, governance, infrastructure, holdings, funding, prestige, publication, and access identify the conditions and residual power sites.
